## 🎮 Game Features

### Core Gameplay
- **🎯 3D Paintball Gun**: Rotating barrel that follows your mouse cursor with realistic physics
- **🎨 Animated Paintballs**: Colorful projectiles with glowing trails and physics-based movement
- **😀 Moving Emoji Targets**: 20+ different emojis bouncing around the screen
- **💰 Bonus Targets**: Special high-value targets for extra points and strategy
- **💥 Spectacular Paint Splats**: Multi-layered, colorful explosion effects when you hit targets

### Progressive Challenge System
- **📈 Escalating Difficulty**: More targets spawn each level (5 + level number)
- **⏰ Time Pressure**: Timer reduces by 5 seconds each new level
- **⚡ Speed Mechanics**: Emojis gain speed when hitting walls (Rosa's brilliant idea!)
- **🏆 High Score System**: Local leaderboard saves your top 10 achievements

### Visual Excellence
- **🌈 3D Visual Effects**: Shadows, gradients, and depth on all game elements
- **🎨 Colorful Design**: Rosa's signature love for bright, vibrant colors
- **✨ Smooth Animations**: 60 FPS gameplay with fluid motion and transitions
- **🎪 Comic Sans Styling**: Fun, playful typography that matches the game's spirit

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **🎯 Aim**: Move your mouse to control the paintball gun direction
2. **💥 Shoot**: Click anywhere to fire colorful paintballs
3. **🎯 Hit Targets**: 
   - Regular emojis = 3 points
   - Bonus targets = 10 points  
4. **🏁 Clear Levels**: Eliminate all targets to advance
5. **⏰ Beat the Clock**: Race against the decreasing timer
6. **⚡ Strategy**: Avoid letting emojis hit walls too much - they speed up!

## 🎯 Advanced Game Mechanics

### Scoring System
- **Base Targets**: 3 points each (😀😎🤩🥳😈👻🤖🦄🐸🐵🐙🍕🍔🍩🍉🍦🎈🎉💎⭐)
- **Bonus Targets**: 10 points each (💰🍭🎁🍀🧸🍒)
- **Bonus Spawn Rate**: 20% chance per target

### Difficulty Progression
- **Starting Timer**: 60 seconds
- **Level Penalty**: -5 seconds per level (minimum 10 seconds)
- **Target Count**: 5 + current level number
- **Base Speed**: 0.8 + (level × 0.3) + bonus modifier
- **Wall Collision**: +0.2 speed boost with red glow effect

### Visual Feedback Systems
- **Speed Boost Indicator**: Red glow and tint when emojis accelerate
- **Timer Warning**: Orange flash when time reduces between levels  
- **3D Depth Effects**: Shadows, highlights, and perspective transforms
- **Paint Splat Physics**: Randomized multi-layer explosion patterns
